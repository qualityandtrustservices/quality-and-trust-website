What we do

Six services,
one standard.

Every job is finished to the same benchmark: the property looks the way it looked in the listing photos. Nothing less counts as done. Serving Largo and the Tampa Bay beaches.

01

Airbnb Cleaning

Between guests, same day.

The window between checkout and check-in is short and unforgiving. We work to that clock: linens stripped and remade to hotel standard, bathrooms reset, sliding glass doors cleared of salt haze, sand gone from every floor, supplies restocked — and a photo report sent when we lock up.

  • Same-day turnaround Standard
  • Linen change & hotel-style beds Included
  • Completion photo report Included
  • Damage & missing item flagging Included
02

Deep Cleaning

Everything a regular clean never reaches.

The reset that puts a property back to zero. Inside appliances, behind and under furniture, baseboards, vents, grout, cabinet interiors, light fixtures and window tracks — the places that quietly build up for a year and then show up in a guest review.

  • Inside oven, fridge & cabinets Included
  • Grout, tracks & vents Included
  • Baseboards & fixtures Included
  • Recommended 2× a year
03

Post-Construction

Dust has a way of coming back.

Construction dust settles three times before it stops. We clean in stages for exactly that reason — rough debris first, then fine particulate from every surface and cavity, then a final detail pass on glass, fixtures and floors once the air has fully settled. Adhesive, paint flecks and protective film removed.

  • Three-stage dust protocol Standard
  • Residue, film & paint removal Included
  • Final glass & fixture detail Included
  • Move-in ready handover Guaranteed
04

Regular Cleaning

Same team, same day, every week.

Weekly, biweekly or monthly service on a fixed schedule with a consistent crew — so nobody has to re-explain the property, the quirks of the lock, or where the supplies live. Consistency is the whole product here, and there is no long-term contract to sign.

  • Weekly / biweekly / monthly Your choice
  • Same assigned crew Guaranteed
  • Fixed day & time slot Included
  • No long-term contract Cancel anytime
05

Residential

Homes, condos, move in & move out.

Standard whole-home service on a one-off or as-needed basis, including move-in and move-out cleans for tenants, owners and realtors. Kitchens and bathrooms sanitized, floors and surfaces throughout, beds made, trash out — the place left the way you would want to walk into it.

  • Move in / move out Available
  • Kitchens & bathrooms sanitized Included
  • All floors & surfaces Included
  • Eco-friendly products On request
06

Commercial

After hours, before anyone notices.

Offices, short-term rental buildings, common areas and small retail. Scheduled outside business hours so the space is spotless when the doors open, with a documented checklist every visit and a single point of contact who actually answers the phone.

  • After-hours scheduling Standard
  • Documented checklist per visit Included
  • Common areas & restrooms Included
  • Licensed & insured crew Verified
