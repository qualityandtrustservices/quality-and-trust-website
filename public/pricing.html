The fine print

How we work.

  1. Prices assume the property is in reasonable condition for its service type. If we arrive and the scope is materially different from what was booked, we call before starting — never after finishing.
  2. Minimums apply. Any job quoting below the service minimum is billed at the minimum.
  3. Cancellations with more than 24 hours notice are free. Under 24 hours is billed at 50%. A locked door with no access is billed in full.
  4. Multi-property hosts receive volume pricing. Tell us how many units you manage and we build a rate against the portfolio, not the individual clean.
  5. We are licensed, insured and bonded. Certificates are provided on request before the first service.
  6. If something is not right, tell us within 24 hours and we come back and fix it at no charge. That is the whole guarantee — there is no form to fill out.
  7. Rates are held for 12 months from the date on this sheet for recurring and portfolio clients.
